# 지문 3 원문 — Nozick's Entitlement Theory of Distributive Justice

출처: 마석고등학교 「외부지문 2 (handout)」 4~5쪽. 마이클 샌델 『정의란 무엇인가』 계열 텍스트.
**게임의 철학적 근거는 이 텍스트에 한정한다.** 원전(Anarchy, State, and Utopia) 전체나 다른 이론을 임의로 끌어오지 않는다.

---

In Anarchy, State, and Utopia (1974), the American philosopher Robert Nozick offers a
philosophical defense of libertarian principles—the idea that individual freedom comes before
everything else—and a challenge to familiar ideas of distributive justice. He begins with the
claim that individuals have rights "so strong and far-reaching" that "they raise the question of
what, if anything, the state may do." He concludes that "only a minimal state, limited to
enforcing contracts and protecting people against force, theft, and fraud, is justified. Any
more extensive state violates persons' rights not to be forced to do certain things, and is
unjustified." Prominent among the things that no one should be forced to do is help other
people. Taxing the rich to help the poor forces the rich to help against their will. It violates
their right to do what they want with the things they own.

According to Nozick, there is nothing wrong with economic inequality in itself. Simply
knowing that the Forbes 400—the four hundred richest Americans—have billions while others
are penniless doesn't enable you to conclude anything about the justice or injustice of the
arrangement. Nozick rejects the idea that a just distribution consists of a certain pattern—
such as equal income, or equal happiness, or equal provision of basic needs. What matters is
how the distribution came about.

Nozick rejects such patterned theories of justice in favor of those that respect the choices
people make in free markets. He argues that distributive justice depends on two
requirements—justice in initial holdings and justice in transfer. The first asks if the resources
you used to make your money were rightfully yours in the first place. (If you made a fortune
selling stolen goods, you would not be entitled to the money.) The second asks if you made
your money either through free exchanges in the marketplace or from gifts voluntarily given
to you by others. If the answer to both questions is yes, you are entitled to what you have,
and the state may not take it without your consent. Provided no one starts out with
dishonestly gained wealth, any distribution that results from a free market is just, however
equal or unequal it turns out to be.

## 게임이 반드시 지켜야 할 6가지 (요구사항 §2)

1. 경제적 불평등 **자체만으로** 부정의라고 결론 내릴 수 없다. → *What matters is how the distribution came about.*
2. **justice in initial holdings** — 돈을 버는 데 쓴 자원이 애초에 정당하게 자기 것이었는가?
3. **justice in transfer** — 자유로운 교환 또는 자발적인 선물(*gifts voluntarily given to you by others*)로 얻었는가?
4. **최소 국가는 아무것도 안 하는 국가가 아니다.** 계약을 집행하고 force·theft·**fraud**로부터 사람들을 보호한다.
5. 지문 속 노직이 반대하는 것은 **재분배 과세(돕도록 강제하는 것)**다. '모든 세금·모든 국가 활동이 부당하다'로 확대하지 않는다.
6. 정당한 취득 + 자발적 이전이면 결과는 **평등해도 불평등해도** 정의롭다. 노직은 평등 자체나 자발적 기부 자체를 반대하지 않는다.
